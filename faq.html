Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first reading.

How does the reading work? +
You choose a service, pay securely, fill in your details and receive your full PDF reading instantly via email or Telegram. No waiting. No appointments.
Is this based on real Ifa? +
Yes. Every reading is generated from the authentic 256 Odu corpus with correct Yoruba proverbs, ese Ifa verses, Orisa prescriptions and Ebo categories — the same framework a trained Babalawo uses.
How long does delivery take? +
Most readings are delivered within 5 minutes of payment. During high traffic, up to 15 minutes.
What languages are available? +
English, Yoruba, and Nigerian Pidgin. Select your language when placing your order.
Is my information private? +
Completely. Your details are never shared, sold or stored beyond what is needed to deliver your reading.
Can I get a refund? +
If your reading was not delivered, you will receive a full refund. Contact admin with your payment reference.
What is the Ritual Guide? +
An optional add-on that gives you the full Ebo prescription — every ingredient, the step-by-step ritual, the prayer in English and Yoruba, and signs to watch for.
Can I use both Telegram and the web app? +
Yes. Both platforms use the same AI engine and deliver the same quality reading. Use whichever is more convenient.
Do I need to know about Ifa to use this? +
No. The readings are written in clear language that anyone can understand, whether you are new to Ifa or deeply familiar with the tradition.
How do I contact support? +
Message the Telegram admin directly at @OrispeaksAdmin or use the contact form on this site.
